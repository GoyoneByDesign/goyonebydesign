ANITA’S / BRAND REDESIGN
2012 ONWARD

One brand.
Out in the world.

A complete redesign for Anita’s New Mexico Style, with a distinctive red chile apostrophe at the heart of its identity.

The idea

Michael’s red chile apostrophe gives the name an unmistakable connection to New Mexico Style. The identity extends beyond the logo into a consistent family of real-world applications.

The scope

  • Logo & brand identity
  • Menus & uniforms
  • Restaurant signage
  • Truck & van wraps
  • Takeout packaging
  • Bottled water design
