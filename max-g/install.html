Add MAX-G to your device

01—04

Use Safari on your iPhone or iPad. If this page opened inside another app, copy the MAX-G link below and paste it into Safari.

  1. 1

    Open MAX-G in Safari

    Tap Open MAX-G to open the app on this website.

  2. 2

    Open Safari’s Share menu

    Look for the square with an upward arrow . Depending on your Safari layout, first tap More (…).

  3. 3

    Choose Add to Home Screen

    Scroll through the Share options; on iPad, More may reveal the option. If shown, turn on Open as Web App.

  4. 4

    Tap Add. Say hello.

    Keep the name MAX-G, then tap Add. Open MAX-G using its blue icon on your Home Screen.

Repeat on each iPhone or iPad. Apple asks you to add the app yourself; a website button cannot complete these steps for you.

Open MAX-G in an up-to-date Chrome browser on your Android phone or tablet.

  1. 1

    Open MAX-G

    Visit MAX-G on this website in Chrome.

  2. 2

    Open the browser menu

    Tap Chrome’s three-dot menu (⋮).

  3. 3

    Choose Install app

    Depending on your browser version, the option may be Add to Home screen, followed by Install.

  4. 4

    Launch MAX-G

    Confirm the browser’s installation prompt, then open the MAX-G icon on your device.

If installation isn’t offered, MAX-G remains available in your browser. Your browser controls installation availability.

A little help getting settled

Will MAX-G work without internet?

After a successful online visit, the app interface can reopen from its offline cache. Local AI and local voices need their own completed downloads and compatible hardware. Live weather, search and online services need an internet connection. Your device may clear cached downloads when storage is low.

Can I use every Mac feature on my iPhone?

Chat, supported voice features, weather, places, animations and browser tools share the same app. Availability depends on your device and permissions; local AI also needs a compatible GPU and enough memory. Voice cloning, connected-account automation and direct computer controls currently require the Mac companion on that Mac. Your phone does not automatically connect to your Mac’s local services.

In MAX-G, open Settings → This device for a capability check.

How do I hear MAX-G?

Open MAX-G and use its sound controls to enable and test voice on this device. Turn up media volume and check whether audio is going to headphones or Bluetooth. Some voices require a separate download. If browser dictation isn’t available, use the microphone on your iPhone keyboard to dictate into the text box.

Is this an App Store download?

This is MAX-G’s free website-installed app. It opens from your Home Screen in its own app window. No App Store account, configuration profile or unsigned installation file is needed.
