VOICE ON THIS DEVICE

Let’s get the sound working

First, play a short speaker test. It needs no voice download or microphone. This also resets MAX-G’s audio connection after a call or screen lock.

Turn up your media volume, then tap Play speaker test.

  1. If the tone is silent, check media volume and the audio destination in iPhone Control Center. Sound may be going to Bluetooth headphones or another device.
  2. Turn off Silent Mode if needed, keep MAX-G visible, and try the speaker test again.
  3. If the tone works but speech does not, watch the voice status above. The natural voice downloads separately on each device; its first preparation can take a few minutes.

Your iPhone has its own voice settings and downloads. A cloned voice needs the paired Mac companion; it does not automatically follow you to your phone.

Voice Studio also offers installed local system voices if this device cannot prepare a natural voice. No cloud voice service is used.
