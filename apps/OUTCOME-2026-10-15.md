5. View4Real: native interaction, safe monitor reset/restore, actual display compatibility, signing and packaging. Model tests do not establish human vision benefit or replace prescribed eyewear.
6. JustMyPick: locate/reconcile matching 0.38 source before native changes; reversible account/mail tests require established consent and access.
