View4Real: evidence classification

- Previously reviewed, not new discoveries: Dual-Input Neural Networks for Personalized Image Precompensation and Binocular Visual Distortion Correction on Display. Direct full-text retrieval again returned 429 and 403 respectively. Search-indexed publisher material is not a substitute for independent full-method verification. No new implementation or prescription range follows.
- New to this log, not newly published: Gaze-adaptive neural pre-correction for mitigating spatially varying optical aberrations in near-eye displays, Array volume 29, March 2026. Indexed primary publisher text describes gaze-conditioned, spatially varying PSFs, simulation and camera measurements through a VR optical system. Reported 11 ms inference used an RTX 4090 for a 280×280 region; this is not measured performance on View4Real's target devices. Direct publisher retrieval was blocked (403; DOI redirect unavailable), so this review does not claim complete independent verification. Classify as special-optics/background research, not ordinary-monitor clinical correction or demonstrated human reading benefit. Code/model/dataset reuse rights were not established. No model, weights, eye tracker or hardware was acquired.
- English, Japanese and Chinese queries did not establish a verified new ordinary-screen result warranting integration in this bounded pass. This is not an exhaustive absence claim.

Retained evaluation requirement: a shared ordinary screen cannot independently deliver arbitrary left/right images without an eye-separation mechanism. A joint binocular objective is not independent per-eye control. Prescription values alone do not calibrate the actual optical point-spread function, pupil, accommodation, distance or worn correction. Bounded display intensities, noise, contrast loss, ringing and clipping must be measured rather than hidden by image-quality averages.

Before an optical change, require held-out synthetic PSFs and distance/pupil/interocular mismatch cases; compare bounded linear-light output with normal text at matched size, enlargement and ordinary sharpening. Report each eye and worst-eye regression, Latin/CJK readability, clipping, artifacts, target-device time and memory. Preserve semantic text, zoom/reflow, original-image comparison and neutral reset. These are evaluation gates, not tests run in this review. No eyesight correction, safety, supported prescription range or replacement for prescribed glasses is established.
