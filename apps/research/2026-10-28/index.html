View4Real: international evidence, not a replacement solver

Read the existing reader-adaptability and research-context records first. Prior Pyolimp, ChromaCorrect, FIU, Montalto, Tanaka/Kawano and Berkeley leads are not new discoveries.

  • Kyushu Institute of Technology / JST Japanese slides, December 9, 2021: simulated visual reproduction and image-quality metrics do not establish human eyesight correction. The slides identify human experiments as future work and disclose patent application 2021-014469. This is additional evidence within an existing research family, not a new 2026 result or reuse/license clearance.
  • Chinese journal abstract: fast deconvolution for myopia/hyperopia in head-mounted displays: the HMD setting and abstract-level algorithm description are not ordinary-monitor or independent per-eye validation. No reproducible implementation was established here; publication recency is not claimed.
  • Dual-input neural image precompensation: revisited publisher-indexed text describes normal-vision participants judging simulated retinal images, not correcting their own eyesight. Direct full-text retrieval returned 429; no experiment reproduced. Keep held-out PSF and model-mismatch tests as prerequisites rather than relying only on matched-model scores.
  • Nagoya Institute university record for binocular display correction: publication is established, but full publisher PDF retrieval returned 403. Participant protocol and ordinary-display hardware efficacy remain unverified in this review.

Decision: retain the current bounded optical laboratory and neutral reader defaults. A shared ordinary display is not independent left/right image control; prescription alone does not calibrate all optical conditions. Any later candidate must compare normal, enlarged and sharpened baselines at matched display constraints, report each eye and worst-eye regression, clipping, contrast, runtime and PSF/pupil/distance mismatch, and be rejected if worse. Real-browser reader acceptance, calibrated optical and human validation remain separate gates. No supported prescription range, clinical safety or glasses-replacement claim follows.

Do not infer that Time Travel alone satisfies these application invariants. The existing source remains disabled and undeployed; no operational data currently requires migration by this review.
