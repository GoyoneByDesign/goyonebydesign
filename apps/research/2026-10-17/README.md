### Older text-specific comparison: SharpView Font

**IEEE VR, March 2024; newly checked in this review, not newly published.** SharpView Font uses constrained total-variation optimization and a text-sharpness measure. Evaluation used simulation and a camera through an optical see-through AR setup. Section 7 explicitly states there was no formal user study. The highest reported sharpness used **90% contrast loss, +4.75 D defocus and a 5 mm pupil**. This is a useful warning against ranking filters by a sharpness metric alone; it does not demonstrate improved reading for prescription-matched people on ordinary screens. [Author publication record](https://ed-swan.github.io/publications/b2hd-IEEEVR24.html), [full paper](https://ed-swan.github.io/publications/papers/2024_Arefin-et-al_SharpView-Font_IEEEVR.pdf)

**Decision:** do not bundle the font or add a solver. The paper carries IEEE copyright restrictions, and no redistributable font/code license or production runtime was verified. If its idea is studied later, use original synthetic text and record retained contrast, clipping and halos alongside every sharpness/error measure. Paper access does not establish asset redistribution rights.

## Comparison against the actual View4Real baseline

The release-source baseline was checked against the limitations and negative cases already recorded in the [October 16 public research review](https://www.goyonebydesign.com/apps/research/2026-10-16/#recommended-next-tests-and-acceptance-baseline). The ordinary-screen research remains a simplified mathematical model, not a calibrated clinical model or a binocular-fusion simulation. Private source details are not reproduced here.

An ordinary screen presents one shared image with bounded nonnegative radiance. It cannot invert optical-transfer-function zeros or independently address the two eyes without additional optics. Prescription entry alone does not calibrate pupil, accommodation, display spectrum, higher-order aberrations or binocular perception.

The prior public review already records an inverse-filter case that worsens one mismatched eye and a matched-eye case where ordinary sharpening performs better. Retain these negative outcomes. Earlier numerical checks and the published native software checks establish only their documented mathematical/software properties.

**Next bounded research candidate:** extend the existing static synthetic benchmark with independent held-out PSFs and original Latin/CJK text/shapes at fixed physical scale. Keep normal rendering, ordinary sharpening and accessible enlargement baselines; do not compare enlarged text against an inequivalent small-text image metric. Report each eye separately, retained contrast, clipping, halos, model mismatch, runtime/memory and cancellation. Optical-bench forward validation remains a missing gate before any benefit claim. No prescription, eye image, camera feed or personal document is needed for the synthetic comparison.
