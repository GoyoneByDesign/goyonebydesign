FAQ

Good questions before you install.

Does View4Real replace eyeglasses?

No. View4Real is not a medical device and does not physically replace prescribed eyewear, contact lenses, diagnosis, treatment, or care from a licensed eye-care professional. The software is still in development and is intended to tune screen comfort and readability only. Users accept the risks of using development software.

Does it work outside the app?

The MVP stores local comfort profiles and can apply in-app brightness changes on iPhone while the app is open. Deeper Windows display, scaling, and text-rendering integrations are planned where operating systems and hardware support them.

How do I switch settings?

Open View4Real and choose a saved profile such as Day Mode, Night Mode, Reading Mode, No Glasses Mode, or Computer Glasses Mode.

Can I pay for a plan today?

No. Plan selection is currently a placeholder for licensing architecture. Subscription billing is planned for a later development phase.
