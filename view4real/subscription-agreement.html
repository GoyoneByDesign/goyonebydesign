View4Real Subscription Placeholder

View4Real does not process payments yet. This page outlines planned licensing structure and should be reviewed by a qualified attorney before any live subscription launch.

Planned Plan Types

The MVP prepares placeholders for a Free Plan, Device Expansion Plan, and Premium Team Plan. Prices and limits may change before launch.

Future Billing

Future paid subscriptions may be processed through a secure billing provider such as Stripe. View4Real should not collect or store raw card numbers, CVV codes, or bank account numbers inside the app.

Receipts and Renewal Notices

When billing is added, customers should receive payment receipts, renewal reminders, and access to cancellation or subscription management controls.

Cancellation and Pause

Future paid members should be able to cancel future renewals through the billing portal or support workflow. Pause controls may be considered in a later release.

Medical Limitation

View4Real is a display comfort utility. It is not a medical device, does not provide medical advice, and does not replace prescribed eyewear, contact lenses, diagnosis, treatment, or care from a licensed eye-care professional.

Development Status and Risk Acceptance

View4Real is still in development. The software is intended to help tune screen comfort and readability, but results may vary based on the customer's prescription, monitor, device settings, lighting, hardware support, and individual eye health. By downloading, installing, subscribing to, or using View4Real, the customer understands and accepts the risks of using development software.

The customer should stop using View4Real if they experience eye strain, headache, dizziness, blurred vision, discomfort, or any other concern, and should consult a licensed eye-care professional for medical or vision-related advice.
