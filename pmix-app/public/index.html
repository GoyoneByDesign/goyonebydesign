How to use
  1. Export Product Mix from Toast for each location and period. Include modifiers if you want Basket / Platter / Meal. Upload all the Excel, CSV or PDF files together.
  2. Check the location, dates and source scope for each file. Toast Excel files use the All levels sheet; Summary and Items are checked, not added again. Assign dining options for menus that do not identify CO, DI, DD or CT. Unassigned menus stay in the combined total.
  3. For PDFs, review the recognized row types and numbers against the original PDF. Scanned pages use English OCR. Fix any recognition mistakes and confirm the review before adding the file.
  4. Choose locations, dining options, menu groups and items. Matching item names are combined across menus. Use separate dining options for the channel breakdown. Distinct products with the same name can be kept separate by clearing Combine matching item names.
  5. Dates and times can only filter at the detail available in the source. For an aggregated file, choose a day part only if that source was already exported for that day part. A normal Product Mix export has no individual sale timestamps.
  6. Choose Letter, Legal or Ledger paper and portrait or landscape, then export Excel or PDF. The footer starts with Downloads; change its label if needed. All report exports use black and gray with MG beside the date.
  7. Use Save report to back up your imported reports or move them to another device. Report files stay on this device. Confirmed layout and dining-option mappings are saved to your private account for reuse on other devices.
